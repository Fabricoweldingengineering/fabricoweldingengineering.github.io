What we do

Full-service steel, under one roof

From a sketch on the back of a drawing to a finished structure bolted into place, we handle every stage in-house — so quality and programme stay in our control.

Structural Steelwork

Beams, columns, frames and connections for residential and commercial builds — fabricated to your engineer's drawings and finished ready for site.

  • Beams & columns
  • Portal frames
  • Steel connections

Welding

MIG, TIG and stick (SMAW) welding across steel, stainless and aluminium — precise, repeatable and visually inspected to standard.

  • MIG
  • TIG
  • Stick / SMAW
  • Aluminium

Design & Engineering

3D CAD modelling and structural calculation in-house. We engineer for manufacture, validate the loads, and produce clean fabrication drawings.

  • 3D CAD
  • Structural calcs
  • DFM

Site Installation

Mezzanine floors, staircases, access platforms, overhead cranes, handrails and guarding — installed on site by our own directly-employed team.

  • Mezzanines
  • Staircases
  • Platforms
  • Cranes

Containers & Fabrication

Bespoke steel containers built from scratch, plus conversions and modifications of standard containers to a custom design — alongside frames, enclosures and one-off metalwork.

  • Bespoke containers
  • Conversions
  • Custom metalwork

Finishing & Maintenance

Hot-dip galvanising and paint/powder-coat finishing for a durable result, plus planned and reactive on-site maintenance and repair.

  • Galvanising
  • Painting
  • On-site repair

Who we are

We design it, then we build it

Fabrico Welding & Engineering is a steel fabricator based at Elms Business Park in Swadlincote, on the Derbyshire–Leicestershire border. We pair real engineering depth with a hands-on workshop, so your steel is designed, checked and built by one accountable team.

Led by a Chartered Engineer, our team takes work from first principles through to a finished, installed product. That means fewer hand-offs, tighter tolerances, and a partner who can solve problems rather than just cut metal.
